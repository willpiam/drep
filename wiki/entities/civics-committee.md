# Civics Committee (Intersect)

The **Civics Committee** is an [[entities/intersect|Intersect]] governance body focused on Cardano civics, constitutional process, and Constitutional Committee operations. It participates in approving governance actions that affect on-chain governance structure and parameters.

## Position (committee-min-size proposal)

Approved submission of [[proposals/committee-min-size-7-to-5]] on **2026-03-13** ([[sources/reduce-committeeminsize-7-to-5]]).

## 2026 Constitutional Committee election

A **Constitutional Committee election working group** under the Civics Committee designed the 2026 election that [[proposals/update-constitutional-committee-2026]] endorses on-chain: constitution-compliant process, open candidate registration, DRep stake-weighted voting (up to four approvals), and independently audited results (DQuadrant). Intersect submitted the Update Committee action as author.
