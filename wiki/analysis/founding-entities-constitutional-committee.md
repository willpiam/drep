# Founding entities and the Constitutional Committee

**From query:** 2026-08-31  
**Questions:** Is anyone from EMURGO / the Cardano Foundation going to be on the committee?

## Answer

**No founding-entity seat** is on the [[concepts/constitutional-committee|Constitutional Committee]] after [[proposals/update-constitutional-committee-2026]] enacts. That includes [[entities/cardano-foundation|Cardano Foundation]], [[entities/emurgo|EMURGO]], and [[entities/input-output-group|IOG]]. [[entities/intersect|Intersect]] also has no CC seat.

Post-enactment roster (7): [[entities/marek-mahut|Marek Mahut]], [[entities/cardano-curia|Cardano Curia]], [[entities/philip-disarro|Philip DiSarro]], [[entities/leandros-bsp|Leandros BSP]], Eastern Cardano Council, Tingvard, Ace Alliance. None is a founding-entity or Intersect seat. Intersect's 2026 candidate summaries list the four election winners as **individuals** or a **community consortium**.

| Entity | Interim CC (appointed) | Stood in 2025 elected CC | 2026 candidate | Post-2026-update seat |
|--------|------------------------|--------------------------|----------------|------------------------|
| Cardano Foundation | Yes | No | No | No |
| EMURGO | Yes | No | No | No |
| IOG | Yes | No | No | No |
| Intersect | Yes | No | No | No |

Ahead of the **2025** fully elected CC, Intersect reported that Intersect, IO, the Cardano Foundation, and EMURGO would **not stand**, ending those appointed seats.

**Gap:** public election materials do not disclose every consortium individual's employer. No published candidate or remaining-member profile identifies a Cardano Foundation, EMURGO, or IOG employee. That is not a signed non-affiliation from each person.

Founding entities still appear elsewhere in governance: CF on **TRSC Oversight** and CCI Pentad; EMURGO as a **DRep** (explorer: abstain on this Update Committee action) and CCI co-proposer; IOG as treasury proposer and CCI co-proposer. Those roles are not CC membership.
